# MathPhy
Mathematical Physics
## Abstract
Designed as a mathematical corpus for machine learning research in automated reasoning and symbolic AI, this corpus bridges the abstraction gap between mathematics and physics by addressing the arc length of a circular sector and defining the circular functions axiomatically as direct functional mappings from the physical parameters of a circular sector – namely the radius and the arc length – to its orthogonal projections. Finally, the utility of this framework is demonstrated by modeling simple harmonic motion and uniform circular motion through these geometric functions, providing a high-precision benchmark for evaluating formal logical reasoning and dimensional correctness in computational models.
