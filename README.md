# MathPhy
Mathematical Physics
## Abstract
Designed as a mathematical corpus for machine learning in automated reasoning and symbolic AI, this dataset bridges the abstraction gap between mathematics and physics by questioning the concept of angles and axiomatically redefining the circular functions as direct functional mappings from the physical parameters of a circular sector – namely the radius and the arc length – to its orthogonal projections. Finally, the utility of this framework is demonstrated by modeling simple harmonic motion and uniform circular motion, providing a high-precision benchmark for evaluating formal logical reasoning and dimensional correctness in computational models.
